If you're finding the wait for specials too stressful I have just the thing for you.
Once upon a time there was a TV show called Star Trek, and it inspired legions of fans. Among those fans there were many who wanted to see Captain Kirk and Spock kiss, and lo a new literary format was born. If your preferred fandom is New Zealand politics, worry not. There are legions of authors (6) who have written the stories journalists are too afraid to tell. 

Archive Of Our Own (Ao3) is the place to find fanfiction these days, having absorbed smaller fandom-specific sites over the years. Unfortunately it went down for three weeks while I was writing this review; to my knowledge no one is investigating this for signs of foreign interference. I conducted an extensive search on Ao3 and I also went on a perilous journey to fanfiction.net (think Myspace compared to Instagram). I am judging these stories based on how funny I would find them to send to a group chat with friends. I am also looking to learn, and I will grant bonus points if they would trigger squeamish reactions during a dramatic reading.


# Disqualified
	Red Chris, Blue Chris
This was removed while I was writing this review, which is unfortunate as I rated it highly. In my notes I wrote "This story portrays the Chrises as quarreling lovers. I find this deeply evocative, portraying the complex relationships that form behind closed doors and the lengths secrecy pushes people to. After reading this the next leaders debate was much more interesting". I also want to shout out the best author's note I have ever read: "english isnt my first language and im not actually from nz so sorry if this isnt lore accurate". What compelled them to write this? Do they see unique dramatic potential in this pairing or is it intended to comment on social affairs? We may never know.

# 6
	Oh! Darling (one shots) [Explicit]		https://archiveofourown.org/works/87270431
In this short story collection there are three featuring our Prime Minister, I'll address each individually.

David Seymour
A private meeting with David Seymour is interrupted by Nicola Willis

While the end is funny it lacks the emotional punch it could have had with a bit more buildup. Interestingly the author spells Seymour as Seymore, that could be a reference to Eeyore but if this is intentional it's only a throwaway reference. At least they won't have to worry about Willis walking in on them now.
Verdict: Not Achieved

Chris Hipkins
Christopher Luxon works out his anger, Chris Hipkins is there.

This is an uncomfortable read. Dom/sub roles require a level of trust I find unlikely in politicians. For excellence the author was required to produce "a selection of crafted and controlled writing using language features appropriate to audience and purpose to command attention", and while I struggled at times, I was not bored.
Verdict: Excellence

John Lennon
Following his breakup with Paul McCartney John is distraught, fortunately Christopher Luxon is there to help him

Part of the appeal of fanfiction is that you can skip the characterisation and get to the interesting stuff, but Christopher Luxon of this story bears so little resemblance to Christopher Luxon the politician and it just makes things confusing. If you can accept this version of Christopher Luxon you will get to experience a mildly muddled story about what it feels like to live in a surveillance state of one.
Verdict: Achieved

The decision to misspell words is a biting critique of NCEA, and only highlights the need for reform. I would say AI could do better, but Claude and Gemini refused to produce objectionable work. For those of you who are used to the ABCDE grading system, I would convert the marks for you but I just cannot wrap my head around such a complicated system so you'll just have to manage.
Final Verdict: Achieved

# 5
	 All the World Shall Be Licked (feat: Pliny the Elder)		https://archiveofourown.org/works/63889312
I was excited when I read this title, the literati aspirant love to compare the US to Rome and I was looking forward to some commentary on the commentariat; but the author let me down. Pliny the Elder strikes me as an odd choice given that he died before the empire reached its peak, and he is known for his encyclopaedia rather than his political life, but maybe that broader view is a valuable contrast to the sometimes myopic day-to-day focus of some politicians. None of that matters though, because this story is not intended to be taken seriously. Luxon and Pliny resemble real people only in name, and the plot has all the connective tissue of a limp jellyfish. Luxon's introduction is striking, although if there is a deeper meaning it escapes me: "Christopher Luxon, the famous Formula One racing accountant, blinked at his newfound freedom, shielding his noses despite the dim light. He gingerly clawed his way out of the ruined funnel, foamed slightly at the mouth, and ejected a dead fish from the fish chute on his back. A shriek and Luxon took off, flapping his legs and flying away with his ✨magical accountant powers✨. " Pliny the Elder is such an odd pairing that I find myself looking for a deeper meaning, but I think the joke is on me for trying to find something.

This story can be funny at times, but it missed the randomcore moment by 20 years. Making matters worse, I learnt nothing about ancient Rome. It's only suitable for your group chat if you're friends with a very particular type of millennial.

# 4
	Boys Working on Empty (is that the kinda way to face the burning heat?) [Explicit]		https://archiveofourown.org/works/55263106
This is a classic forbidden romance story, Chris and Chris are dating but they can't let the public know. But then, in one debate Christopher goes too far and so their rivalry gets even more heated. Something has broken and it may never be the same.

GayEgg05 comments "Do we weep without rhythm so we don’t attract the damned?", quoting a line of poetry from MineCraft YouTuber JoeHills. This is the question at the heart of the story, how do you make a relationship work in a world trying to tear you apart? The grammar needs work, however I understand the heat of the moment sometimes overwhelms you.

This is one of the founding texts of genre, unfortunately everything it does has been done better later. I don't believe the average person is interested in reading more than three Christopher Luxon fanfictions, so you can skip this.


# 3
	Thrown's Strategic Ascent [sic]		https://www.fanfiction.net/s/14281404/1/Thrown-s-Strategic-Ascent
This is the sole fanfiction on fanfiction.net, and it's a Star Wars Crossover. There is also a typo in the title, which is odd coming from an author with the name "Thrawn's Number 1 Fan". As I am not a Star Wars expert I consulted a friend who provided this excellent analysis:

Thrawn has been a huge character for Star Wars nerds since some books introduced him in the early 90s, and he's basically the platonic ideal of "what if the villain had none of the usual impulses and cartoonish cruelty of villains, and just clinically analysed every situation and perfectly figured out how to win it", which sounds unbearable and it's kind of incredible that there are so many good Thrawn stories, even if I think his shtick is a bit worn out by now. Sooo, in this fanfiction this is basically a reader conveying "gee golly I love reading these books where the Galactic Empire's most brilliant mind in the entire galaxy is like 'hmm what if i simply did all the thing that make the most sense', our country is so divided between left and right...if only Thrawn came in and just did the things that made sense". In conclusion: Thrawn is definitely the next TOP leader.

Reading this I was struck with melancholy, one question looping in my mind: why must things be bad when they could be good? Ultimately this falls in third place as Christopher Luxon only makes a brief appearance, but your friends may find it amusing to read while they sit on the toilet.

# 2
	Still in a closet [Explicit]		https://archiveofourown.org/works/56275234
This is inspired by Boys Working on Empty, and like the Godfather Part II this story takes what works and adds vivid imagery and deeper, more complicated characters After a heated debate Chris and Christopher find themselves in a broom closet where they relieve some stress. Bipartisanship helps them come to a mutually agreeable conclusion but politics always comes at a cost.

I note that this story has real quotes, I'm glad they've done their research. Political insider Grayggle commented "This actually happened", lending further credence to the theory that this is a work of non-fiction. It's huge if true, and no one would lie on the internet so it must be. To think, the real sequel to Dirty Politics has been sitting in public for years without anyone noticing.

This story understands that we don't need a grand narrative, we just need a moment of real humanity. It provoked an intense emotional reaction, and that's what literature is supposed to do. Still in a Closet gets second place only because I am too weak to handle it.
Send this to your friends at your own risk, it's much better to read it aloud while quite drunk.

# 1
	Across the Aisle		https://archiveofourown.org/works/66054427

Life in politics is difficult, there are endless catered lunches, voters who want you to do things, and regular coup attempts. However it can also unite people, much as Christopher Luxon and David Seymour do in this story. Across the Aisle tells the story of a clandestine romance. David and Chris find the small moments, just enough to keep going, and they don't let rumours stop them loving each other. They make it work when everything is working against them. Until Winston Peters returns.

This is genuinely funny; the author uses metaphors like an MP uses taxpayer money -- frequently and ostentatiously -- which keeps the story on the right side of seriousness. It also has the best grammar yet, which you can't take for granted. While you may think you've found 'errors' they are in fact linguistic innovation by one of our freshest minds. It seems almost unthinkable that we would have a polyamorous head of government, yes queer rights have made progress but there are a lot of people still fighting for their rights. A polyamorous head of government would be radical, but given the results of the election people clearly want new ideas.

It will make you, it's extremely quoteable, and you might learn about coalition negotiations. This would make a great book club read for the week and you can discuss it next Saturday at brunch. I would love for someone to ask Winston Peters what he thinks of this story. You should absolutely send this to your friends, this needs to go all the way to the top.


At the end of this process I decided to look up other leaders going back to Muldoon; there is only a single story tangentially featuring John Key and a brief mention of a Jacinda Ardern in an alternate universe anime fanfiction set in pandemic-era New Zealand. So what makes Christopher Luxon a uniquely inspiring PM for authors when he isn't for the rest of New Zealand? What does he have that John Key and Jacinda Ardern don't?

I find fanfiction is often not written about the most developed characters because there is no space to play, instead people write about characters they can mould to fit their story and they write because they feel an urge to fill a gap. I guess what I'm saying is that Christopher (and Chris) need to get a personality, even if it's just one between two.
